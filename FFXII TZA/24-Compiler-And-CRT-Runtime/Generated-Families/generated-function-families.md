# The generated-function family catalogue

> **Fifty-five shapes, 1,236 functions, one instruction form each.** Not one of them needs reading
> individually, and saying so is the whole value - but only after measuring it, and only down to a
> size where a family row still earns itself.

## 1. What is here and what is not

A group qualifies when **every member decodes to the same instruction sequence** once constants are
dropped, and when it has **ten or more members**.

| | groups | functions |
|---|---:|---:|
| one form, **10 or more** members - **declared here** | **55** | **1,236** |
| one form, fewer than 10 - **not declared** | 132 | 415 |
| of those, **exactly one member** | **70** | 70 |

> **The threshold is a judgement and it is stated so it can be argued with.** A row that covers ten
> identical bodies clearly earns its own existence. **A "family" of one is a function**, and
> declaring 70 of those would inflate the disposition count by exactly the kind of move
> `COMPLETION-PLAN` §7 warns about. The 415 stay in the frontier, visible, and can be picked up by
> whoever explains their neighbours.

**Nothing here is a claim about behaviour.** These rows say *this shape was emitted by a tool, once
per declaration, and a reconstruction that writes the same declarations gets it back for free*.
They say nothing about what any member computes.

## 2. What each row does not disposition

Every members file carries an **external-target census** - call and jump targets that leave the
body, with intra-body branches excluded. It divides into two kinds, and the distinction decides how
much a row is worth:

- **Shared targets** - reached by every member. Those are the family's shared implementation, and
  they stay in the frontier.
- **Member-specific targets** - one per member. A family with those is a family of **wrappers**, and
  **the row disposes of the wrapping, not of what is wrapped.**

`NativeAdapter.ForwardSecondArgumentTailCall`, declared elsewhere, is the clearest case: 82 members
and 82 distinct targets. Eighty-two argument shuffles disposed of; **nothing they shuffle into.**

> The census was re-derived after an earlier version of it counted **intra-body jumps** as external
> targets and reported 108 where the answer was 4. Local branches are excluded here by construction.

## 3. The catalogue

The fifty-five rows are in this page's frontmatter, each naming its member count, body sizes, how
many distinct byte images collapse into the single form, its external-target census, and what it
leaves undispositioned. The member lists are in
[`members/`](members/), one file per family, each repeating that provenance in its own header so a
file separated from this page still carries it.
