## What it lists

The table [paramina-rift-scripts.tsv](paramina-rift-scripts.tsv) has one row per script function of the Paramina Rift
that does something a player can observe. It covers the nine zone scripts and the one scene script of the region, from
`plan_master/us`:

| Script | Kind | Map or scene | Actors | Functions | Rows | Shared helpers |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `pbc_a01` | zone | Fading Vale, field `463` | 20 | 81 | 8 | 5 |
| `pbc_a02` | zone | Head of the Silverflow, field `464` | 27 | 43 | 13 | 0 |
| `pbc_a03` | zone | Freezing Gorge, field `465` | 23 | 37 | 11 | 0 |
| `pbc_b01` | zone | Frozen Brook, field `468` | 29 | 45 | 14 | 0 |
| `pbc_b02` | zone | Icebound Flow, field `469` | 26 | 42 | 13 | 0 |
| `pbc_b03` | zone | Karydine Glacier, field `470` | 30 | 47 | 14 | 0 |
| `pbc_c01` | zone | Path of the Firstfall, field `473` | 27 | 95 | 12 | 5 |
| `pbc_c02` | zone | Spine of the Icewyrm, field `474` | 20 | 30 | 6 | 0 |
| `pbc_c03` | zone | Silverflow's End, field `475` | 31 | 58 | 17 | 1 |
| `pbc_a0300` | scene | event `269` | 18 | 118 | 6 | 0 |

Fading Vale and Path of the Firstfall carry the five helper actors that many zone scripts share: the crystal
(`system_crystal_actor`, `telepoeffectctrl`, `crystaldummyPC`, `saveactor`) and the key-item and message helper
`イベント特殊効果画面`; Silverflow's End carries the key-item and message helper alone. Their functions are described once on
[Shared zone-script actors](shared-zone-script-actors.md) and have no rows here; the Shared helpers column counts
them.

A function is listed when it shows a line, sets or releases a story flag, writes the story counter, gives or takes
items, jumps to another map, makes one of the battle calls below, loads a scene or changes the party.

Functions that only move, animate, wait, play sound or set the camera have no row in this table; each listed row
counts the other functions of its actor in `other_functions`. Every function, these included, has its steps in the
steps table described under [Every step](#every-step). The scene itself, with its dialogue, is on [Paramina Rift
scenes](paramina-rift-scenes.md).

### What the scripts do

- Fading Vale and Icebound Flow: `常駐ディレクター.main` loads the location title `ttl_z0000` (event 215) on the first arrival
  and sets flags `0x0038` and `0x0028`, the location map's exits to Golmore Jungle and The Feywood.
- Fading Vale holds the crystal; refugees and Kiltias there talk of Mt Bur-Omisace ("The Kiltias of Mt Bur-Omisace
  took us in and treated us warmly.").
- Freezing Gorge: while the story counter is below 1973, `常駐ディレクター.init` loads scene `pbc_a0300` (event 269),
  Balthier's and Larsa's talk of peace; `main` plays it, writes 1973 and jumps to `alc_z01` (Archades), field 866.
  When the counter is 1976, after the Archades scene of Gramis and Vayne, `main` fades the gorge in again.
- Freezing Gorge: arriving by entrance 3 sets flag `0x0037`, the location map's exits to Mt Bur-Omisace.
- Frozen Brook: Monid joins the hunt for the Trickster (`常駐ディレクター.main`: "So, you're here after all. Hmph. We may as
  well work together, I suppose."); after it `wanted_kansi.main` removes the foes and Monid leaves for the clan hall
  ("I'll leave the reportin' back to you.").
- Karydine Glacier: `hunt_kansi.main` gives the Mind Trophy and removes the foes of a group. Spine of the Icewyrm and
  Silverflow's End: `wanted_kansi.main` removes the foes of a group, and at Silverflow's End gives a Ring of the
  Light.
- Path of the Firstfall holds the crystal and the Strahl's anchor: talking to `system_anchor_actor` requests
  `ym_shutoraljump.shtjump`, which loads scene `str_z0000` (event 211) and jumps to the field a script variable holds
  ([Strahl scene](strahl-scenes.md)).
- Path of the Firstfall: two wounded Kiltias tell of the feral retriever at the Spine of the Icewyrm and of the wyrm
  Fafnir at Silverflow's End.
- Silverflow's End: `常駐ディレクター.main` sets flag `0x0036`, the location map's exits to the Stilshrine of Miriam.
- Head of the Silverflow, Freezing Gorge, Frozen Brook, Icebound Flow, Karydine Glacier, Path of the Firstfall, Spine
  of the Icewyrm and Silverflow's End hold 10, 5, 8, 8, 10, 1, 3 and 11 treasure chests.

The story counter is the script's global `short` at offset 0, named `シナリオフラグ` in the scripts of this table that write
or test it: every assignment to it is a write and every comparison a test ([story counter
values](story-counter-values.md)).

### The four message verbs reach one window call

The four message verbs all end in `mes` (`0x461`, `0x00348DF0`), which looks the text id up in the issuing script's
section 2 ([EBP text sections](../EBP-Script-Files/ebp-text-sections.md)):

1. `amese` (`0x0034A420`) pushes 0, 0, 0 and -1; `amest` (`0x0034A090`) pushes 0; `ames` (`0x00349BF0`) pushes 1. Each then
   jumps to `0x00349800`, the body of `ames_1fe`.
2. `0x00349800` pops two values (`0x00349827`, `0x00349831`), pushes 0 and 0 (`0x0034983D`, `0x00349847`) and then pushes the
   two values back in their original order (`0x00349851`, `0x0034985B`), so the zeros lie beneath them; it jumps to
   `0x00348DF0` (`0x00349884`).
3. `0x00348DF0` pops eight values. The seventh, the script's second argument, is passed with the current field context
   to `0x00264D60` (`0x00348E6A`-`0x00348E74`), the section 2 lookup, which keeps the low 24 bits of the id.

### Speakers, places and scenes

- The speaker is the second name of each pair the actor's `setnpcname` calls name in `us` `npcdic.bin`. The
  message window opener `0x002E16B0` sets the actor's name flag before the window opens, and `0x00263990` shows the
  second name while that flag is set.
- A field id is named by its map name, entry `id` of `us` `planmapname.bin` (`0x00377B60`), and its region, the
  entry after the map names that `0x003778B0` selects through the field's group in `map_ref.bin` (`0x00264F90`).
- An event id `n` names the scene script at index `4n` of the event file table, which `0x0032FCA0` loads for scene
  managers 1 to 3.

### Every step

The steps table [`paramina-rift-script-steps.tsv`](paramina-rift-script-steps.tsv) has one row for each step of every
function of the ten scripts, in the order the function runs them: each call, request, store, branch, loop, case and
return. It has 6,288 rows.

A row gives where the step sits in the script, the branch it runs under, the verb and its arguments, and what those
arguments name in the game: the actor, the model, the animation and its motion file, the effect file, the event sound,
the camera shot, the message window or the variable. It also gives the frames the step takes, what it waits for, and
the functions it starts on other actors.

| Script | Steps | Script | Steps |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| `pbc_a01` | 963 | `pbc_b03` | 488 |
| `pbc_a02` | 449 | `pbc_c01` | 955 |
| `pbc_a03` | 473 | `pbc_c02` | 288 |
| `pbc_b01` | 590 | `pbc_c03` | 562 |
| `pbc_b02` | 475 | `pbc_a0300` | 1,045 |

The five shared helper actors have their steps once, in [`shared-zone-script-steps.tsv`](shared-zone-script-steps.tsv)
(1,283 rows over 26 templates for 29 functions: the template of a function that only returns, cecbbef6fb, serves the
`init` of イベント特殊効果画面, telepoeffectctrl and crystaldummyPC and the `main` of saveactor). In this table each of their
functions has a single `shared template` row naming the template; Paramina Rift has 68 such rows.

An actor that runs another actor's code would have one `same code` row naming the actor whose steps it runs; this
table has none.

The steps by their `resolution` cell:

| Resolution | Steps |
|---|---:|
| `resolved` | 6,264 |
| empty | 24 |

A row whose `resolution` cell is empty gives the step as the listing writes it: the verb, and the arguments as the
script writes them (`-` for a verb that takes none). Its `addresses`, `timing` and `sync` cells carry what they give
for the step, for example the variable that receives a result, and `-` otherwise.

A scene reads from top to bottom in its director's function. In `pbc_a0300` (event `269`), `PRODUCER.main` places the
party with `setposparty` at one of two points by `file_var_s0_2`, files `MUSIC.music_1_vol` and waits until it ends,
and reads motions 1 and 2 of `pbc_a0300.mot` and voice slot 0, waiting for each. It then hides the party and files
`cut_0` on its cast together, waiting for those of `VAN`, `FRAN`, `BALFLAIR`, `PANNERO`, `LARSAR`, `MAN_01`, `MAN_02`,
`MAN_04`, `WOMAN_01`, `WOMAN_02` and `CHILD_01`, and hides the foes.

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `script` | the script, by its file name |
| `place` | for a zone script, its map (region); for a scene script, `scene, event` and its event id |
| `actor`, `function` | the actor and the function, as the script names them |
| `trigger` | the function's slot (`talk`, `touch`, `touchon`, `across`, `main`, `mapjump`), `init`, or `requested by` and the functions of the same script that request it by name; `-` when neither applies |
| `speaker` | the names `setnpcname` gives the actor, filled only for a row whose lines include a section 2 line |
| `lines` | the section 2 entries the function passes to the message verbs and the ask verbs |
| `line_text` | the `us` text of those entries, in the same order, separated by ` / ` |
| `flags_set`, `flags_released`, `flags_tested` | the flag ids, each with the first sentence of its explanation on [story flag ids](story-flag-ids.md) |
| `counter_set` | each value written to the story counter, with the first sentence of its explanation on [story counter values](story-counter-values.md) |
| `counter_tested` | each comparison of the story counter, as the relation and the decimal value |
| `items` | items received or lost, with their names; `treasure chest` and its record reference for a chest; for a request of the helper, the item the requesting function hands over; a fixed count is written `xN`, and a count the game computes reads `, as many as <expression> gives` |
| `jumps` | each `mapjump`: the target map (region), its field id and the entrance; `field held in` and the variable when the target field is a variable |
| `battles` | `boss gauge shown`, `every foe in a group removed`, `every foe in group 0 removed` |
| `scenes` | each scene script the function loads, with its event id |
| `party` | not used by these scripts: `-` on every row |
| `other_functions` | the actor's functions that have no row |
| `shared_actor` | `-` for an ordinary actor; for a function of a shared helper actor that is listed, whether its body matches the shared one |

In the `line_text` column, a choice list is written as `(choices: ...)`, and a value the game fills in when the line
is shown is written `[value]`.

The script list [paramina-rift-script-list.tsv](paramina-rift-script-list.tsv) holds the first table above, one row
per script:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `script`, `kind`, `place` | the script, `zone` or `scene`, and its map (region) and field id, or its event id |
| `actors`, `functions` | the script's actors and functions, shared helpers included |
| `rows` | the script's rows in the main table |
| `shared_actors` | the shared helper actors the script carries whose functions all match; `differs:` names a carried one that does not |

The steps table [`paramina-rift-script-steps.tsv`](paramina-rift-script-steps.tsv) has one row per step:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `script`, `actor`, `function` | as in the main table |
| `step` | the step's number in its function, from 1, in the order the function's bytes run |
| `at` | where the step's instruction sits: its offset in section 0, the script image, of the shipped `plan_master/us` `.ebp`. For `case`, `default` and `label` it is where their code starts. For `shared template` and `same code` it is where the function or actor starts |
| `line` | the step's line in the script listing, `-` for the `shared template` and `same code` rows |
| `condition` | the tests the step runs under, outermost first, separated by `;`. A case is written `x = v`, a default `x is none of ...`, an else part `not (test)` and a loop body `loop while test` |
| `verb` | the verb the step calls, by its name in the [verb table](../Scripts-And-Script-Commands/verb-table.md), written `Actor.verb` for a call on another actor. Otherwise the kind of step: `if`, `else if`, `while`, `for`, `switch`, `case`, `default`, `break`, `goto`, `label`, `return`, `returnT`, `store`, `shared template` or `same code` |
| `arguments` | the arguments as the script writes them; for `store` it is `variable = value`, and for a branch or loop it is the test |
| `addresses` | what the arguments name in the game: the actor, model, animation and motion file, effect file, event sound, camera shot, message window, request slot or variable |
| `quoted_lines` | for a call that shows dialogue or a system message, each line it shows, written `line N: ` and the us text with its control codes dropped; `-` for every other step |
| `timing` | the frames, fade lengths and speeds the step uses |
| `sync` | what the step waits for, and for a request whether it waits until the function starts, until it ends or not at all |
| `calls` | the functions a request starts, as `actor.function`; for a `goto` into another function, that function |
| `resolution` | `resolved`; `does not ship:` and the fact; or empty |
| `source` | the page or table that gives the meaning, or the function whose body was read |

The shared steps table [`shared-zone-script-steps.tsv`](shared-zone-script-steps.tsv) has the same columns from `at`
on, after these:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `template` | the template id that the `shared template` rows name |
| `actor`, `function`, `step` | the shared actor, its function and the step number |
| `reference` | the script whose copy of the function the steps were read from, and where the function starts there |

In this table, `at` is the step's distance from the function's start, written `+0x..`. Variable names, text ids,
section 3 string offsets and the lines `addresses` names are those of the script named in `reference`. The other
scripts' copies differ only in these.
