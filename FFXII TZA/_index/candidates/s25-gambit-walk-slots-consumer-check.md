**The correction.** `CONTRACT-gambit-walk.md` §7 (OPUS, owner) said that the walk passes *"the target … as a word from a
stack slot initialised to `0xFFFF`, the no-target sentinel"*, and that the third call's second argument is *"the resolved
target word"*. Both are wrong:
- **The word slot `[rsp+0x70]`** (initialised to `0xFFFF` at `00305CCF`–`00305CD6`) is the evaluator's `out`. The evaluator
  stores the decoded **action id** there (`00301500 mov word [r12], ax`), and the walk passes it to `0030F760` as `r8w`
  (`00305DE3`), which is the commit's action id.
- **The dword slot `[rsp+0x30]`** (initialised to 0) is the evaluator's `param`. The evaluator stores the **target id** there
  (`003017E7`), and the walk passes it as `r9d` (`00305DDB`).
- **The third call** passes `movzx edx, word [rsp+0x70]` (`00305E44`), so its second argument is **the action word**.
