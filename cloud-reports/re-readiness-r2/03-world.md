- **Other categories.** These were grepped for every function, global and question a gap names. **Where another
  category's page already answered a gap at pass 1's snapshot**, the verdict is **WRONG-IN-PASS-1**. Pass 1 said it
  gave such pointers, but missed these. Each such answer was checked with `git show df2c9f47:<page>`.

**Citation form.** `path:line`. A path is relative to `FFXII TZA/03-World-Area-Field/` unless it starts with a
category folder (`14-Story-Event-Quest-Hunt/…`) or `_index/`, which are relative to `FFXII TZA/`. Line numbers are those of
snapshot `0dc9cb77`. `OQ` means `_index/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.

**Classes** (for OPEN, NARROWED and NEW items, per the brief):
- **STATIC:** a desk read of the executable or data answers it.
- **RUNTIME:** it needs a live observation.
- **DATA:** it needs a census of shipped game data.
- **DOC:** the answer exists on another page, and the page only lacks the pointer or still prints the overturned text.
- **LIMIT:** the register has retired it as NO ADMISSIBLE SOURCE or OUT OF EVIDENCE BOUNDARY. Nothing can answer it,
  and a port does not need it.
